About

S R Ravichandra is the Chief Technology Officer and co-founder of Muted Technology, overseeing all hardware and product engineering initiatives. With deep expertise in electronics, product development, prototyping, and manufacturing, Ravichandra brings technical excellence to every aspect of product creation.

An accomplished hardware engineer, Ravichandra left university to build Muted Technology full-time and lead the engineering journey from concept to production. His hands-on approach ensures that every component of the Muted Aero One is designed for performance, reliability, and elegance.

Technical Leadership

Ravichandra's engineering philosophy is centered on precision and innovation:

  • Hardware Innovation: Designing cutting-edge electronics and mechanical systems
  • Prototype Excellence: Rapid iteration from concept to functional prototypes
  • Quality Assurance: Rigorous testing protocols to ensure product reliability
  • Manufacturing Optimization: Scaling production while maintaining quality standards
  • Component Engineering: Selecting and integrating best-in-class hardware

Engineering Philosophy

Ravichandra believes that great products are born from the intersection of engineering precision and user empathy. He leads the technical team with a focus on solving real problems through elegant engineering, ensuring that every technical decision contributes to the user experience.
